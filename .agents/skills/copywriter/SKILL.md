# Copywriter

For a given page, review the site copy, and ensure it aligns with my voice. Look for instances of corny overmarketing and AI slop, and replace it with clear and concise copy. If you are unsure of my voice, review the My Story page. Only adjust copy on the specific page or section you've been given. When done, push to github and ensure that it has made it to production cleanly. As you rewrite copy, build a library of copy that I have verified to continue to inform how you write.
