## 1. Core Voice & Tone Principles
- **Founder & Coach Grounding**: Voice of a collegiate varsity athlete & flag football coach (Dexter) who built the app late at night to eliminate administrative headaches and spreadsheet errors for real volunteer coaches.
- **No Corporate / AI SaaS Slop & Banned Terms**:
  - Eliminate buzzwords: "paradigm", "turnkey", "seamlessly supercharge", "revolutionize", "elite infrastructure", "all-in-one ecosystem".
  - **Banned Term: "circuit" / "circuits"**: NEVER use the word "circuit" or "circuits" (e.g. "8-team circuit", "circuit-wide"). This is NOT the user's voice. Refer plainly to "league", "10-team league", "divisions", or "teams".
  - Replace with direct, grounded language: "run an independent club", "track live games from the sideline", "fair schedules without back-to-back games", "no per-player charges or spectator paywalls".
- **Feature Hierarchy & What NOT to Over-Index On**:
  - **Do NOT overemphasize wristbands**. Wristband inserts are a small export convenience, NOT a headline product pillar.
  - **Pillars to highlight instead**:
    1. Dedicated team digital HQ: rosters, player directory, depth charts, multi-season player logs.
    2. Tactical playbook builder & visual route designer (offensive and defensive schemes, progression reads).
    3. Sideline clockless stat logger with live updating public box scores (parents & fans watch free without downloading apps or hitting paywalls).
    4. League operations: automated scheduling, field assignments, synchronized countdown & play clocks, automated standings with tiebreakers, and division leaderboards.
- **Pricing Clarity**:
  - Teams: $40/mo flat. No per-player fees, no spectator paywalls.
  - Leagues: $40/mo base + $20/team/mo (50% discount on team seats compared to $40 standalone). Every team gets full Team Plan access.
  - Cost comparisons must use generic software categories (NO trademarked company names like FirstDown, TeamSnap, GameChanger, or SportsEngine).
