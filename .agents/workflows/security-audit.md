description: Security
---

Explore the flagos.ca site from the perspective of a bad actor trying to cyberattack me. Look for data vulnerabilities, poor auth flows, and other exploitations. Do not do any real harm to the site, just find places that could be harmed, and create a report. Once you've created the report, pause, review it, and think deeply about the most secure way to resolve it without harming functionality or creating 
latency. As you increase security, build a tradeoff analysis.
